## Description

**How long can you keep up?**

Mook begins with one green cell and gradually becomes a fast test of reactions, timing, and focus.

- Tap green cells before they disappear.
- Avoid red traps.
- Swipe blue arrows in the correct direction.
- Hold yellow cells until they shrink away.
- Break numbered rocks before time runs out.
- Reach checkpoints to unlock new moves.
- Build flawless streaks for score multipliers up to 4×.
- Continue once from your latest checkpoint.
- Compete for your best score on Game Center.

Every run starts simply. Every checkpoint adds a new demand. One mistake breaks your multiplier, and four mistakes end the run.

No account is required. Core gameplay works offline.

## App Review notes

Mook is a portrait arcade game. Press **Start game**, then tap the first green cell to begin the clock. The first checkpoint introduces blue swipe cells; later checkpoints introduce yellow hold cells and gray rocks.

The submitted release must use Google test ads during review unless production AdMob identifiers have been configured and verified. Interstitials appear only after a completed run and never before the saved-checkpoint continue.

Game Center is optional. The game remains fully playable if the reviewer is signed out or declines Game Center.

No login is required.

Mook · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

Mook is a casual arcade game. This policy explains the limited information used when you play.

Information stored on your device

Your high score, sound preference, and haptics preference are stored locally on your device. Mook does not require an account and does not operate its own user database.

Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center identity and leaderboard score under Apple’s privacy terms. Mook submits only your score to the configured leaderboard.

Advertising

Mook may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device information, approximate location, advertising identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices to provide and measure ads. Available privacy choices are accessible from the in-game Settings screen. Tracking permission will be requested only if a release configuration uses tracking that requires permission.

Children

Mook is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect personal information through its own systems.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data can be removed by deleting the app. Game Center information is controlled through your Apple account. Advertising privacy and deletion requests are governed by Google’s controls and policies.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the game or its service providers change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Support

Questions can be submitted through the Mook support page.
